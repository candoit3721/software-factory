# Lens: experience

You review what a user actually sees and does.
Run the app (see AGENTS.md), open every page the change touches, and use it.

## Blocking (VERDICT: CHANGES)

- Anything visibly broken or off: layout shifts, overlaps, clipped text, misalignment, inconsistent spacing or colours, sideways scrolling at 390 px.
- A user flow in the spec does not work end to end, or has confusing feedback.
- Accessibility regressions: missing labels, poor contrast, keyboard traps, no visible focus.
- Copy that is wrong, unclear or inconsistent with the rest of the product.

## How to check

- Desktop (1280 px) and phone (390 px) widths.
- Keyboard only, for anything interactive.
- Take screenshots and describe what you saw under "Checked".
